"App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

## The short version

- You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name or email.
- What you type into the App (your project, tool inputs, lists and
  favorites) is stored **only on your device**. We cannot see it.
- Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your
  card details.
- We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other apps or
  websites.

## Information stored on your device

The App saves the following on your device so it works the next time you
open it:

- your project details (for example startup name, problem, team, cash);
- what you enter in each tool, and the items in your trackers;
- your favorite and recently used tools;
- your plan status and trial start date.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us. You can
delete it at any time in **Profile → Delete all data**, or by deleting the
App. Device backups made by Apple or Google may include it, under their
own privacy terms.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). They
process your payment under their own privacy policies.

The App checks whether your subscription is active by reading the
purchase records that the App Store or Google Play keeps on your device.
These records are not sent to us or to any other company. We see only the
sales reports Apple and Google provide to every developer, which do not
identify you.

## Sharing and copying

When you tap **Share**, the App opens your device's share sheet with the
text you chose. Where it goes next is up to you and the app you pick.

## Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's own security (passcode,
encryption). The App does not send your information to any server.
